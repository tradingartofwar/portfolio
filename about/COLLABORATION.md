# Synergistic Intelligence and contribution

Synergistic Intelligence is the sustained Human–AI operating method behind this portfolio. Troy Vance Williams and AI developed complementary roles, then external structures for continuity and coordination as the work exposed the limits of what one human could carry. The method and its evolution are themselves a central engineering subject.

[Read the development history](../case-studies/SYNERGISTIC_INTELLIGENCE.md): from the original productivity and project-switching ambitions, through the human bottleneck and Git-backed project systems, to distributed collaboration and the prediction that AI will recede from ordinary attention.

## Division of responsibility

Vance supplies direction, meaning, intuition, judgment, and authority. AI supplies technical execution, computation, structural continuity, research support, and orchestration. AI can also propose questions, interpretations, and objections within the delegated task. Human authority does not make a factual claim true; evidence can correct both participants.

“AI carries structure; the human carries meaning” is a useful shorthand. Meaning includes what matters; retaining authority over it does not require the human to remember or discover everything alone. AI can contribute to framing and interpretation within the partnership. External structure supports continuity, source recovery, and return to unfinished work.

Its practical test is whether the arrangement expands useful participation while preserving the human's ability to understand, judge, authorize, and correct consequential work. As the systems mature, the intended experience makes less of that supporting machinery demand attention.

## Attribution in this portfolio

The [EXP-001 source report](https://github.com/tradingartofwar/representation-science/blob/b8750dee72d5cc9a76d5dbee59077743cee735a9/experiments/EXP-001-results-2026-10-03.md) explicitly records that AI authored the protocol, implementation, checking, and interpretation with the investigator's direction. The [Lonely Runner introduction](https://github.com/tradingartofwar/the-lonely-runner-conjecture/blob/c8a287f52ef0066d2ba6247094bdeba023825081/README.md) describes the inquiry as a collaboration between Vance and an AI partner.

Accordingly, this portfolio presents **work produced through Human–AI collaboration**. It does not attribute every implementation detail, mathematical argument, or line of code to unaided human work. A commit author is a provenance clue, not a complete account of intellectual contribution.

## Decisions that make the contribution visible

**Finding a buildable route.** In his [origin account](../case-studies/HOW_WE_STARTED.md), Vance recalls reframing a lost experience of continuity as a glimpse of the future. After roughly two weeks of unsuccessful attempts to arrange a replacement, he asked whether an existing open-source chatbot could provide a starting point. GPT supplied a GitHub lead and interpreted unfamiliar technical language. He recalls a working local chatbot after three days of collaboration. This is his retrospective account of complementary contributions; the original implementation has not been recovered.

**Opening a new research direction.** The public origin account records AI being invited into framing, followed by Vance creating Representation Science as a separate inquiry. The [question-quality note](https://github.com/tradingartofwar/representation-science/blob/b8750dee72d5cc9a76d5dbee59077743cee735a9/notes/WORKING_NOTE_QUESTION_QUALITY_UPSTREAM_CONTROL_2026_10_04.md) explicitly discusses Vance's authorization for that participation. AI contributed comparison across episodes and formalization. [The case study](../case-studies/WHEN_THE_QUESTION_CHANGED.md) follows this decision into an experiment that corrected a stronger interpretation.

**Choosing where to stop.** Vance reports ending work on the early Aichi receptionist because it no longer appeared to be a product the future needed. The [Aichi case](../case-studies/AICHI_TRAJECTORIES.md) preserves the trajectory insight and the product judgment. This is an attributed operational account, not a measured market forecast.

These are concrete contributions to what work gets pursued and how its purpose is understood. The experiment's internal-reconstruction correction is attributed at the level the source supports: AI-authored work under investigator direction. Its exact human intervention history is not supplied.

Future cases should retain the initial question, the specific human choice, AI's material work, the resulting evidence, and the changed decision. A commit author alone cannot establish that history.

## Review limits

The October 14 audit was performed by an AI assistant at Vance's request. Fresh execution, exact arithmetic, alternative algorithms, and artifact comparisons support bounded reproducibility. They are not independent human review, formal verification, or an external research endorsement.

The broader proposition that this collaboration enables outcomes neither participant could achieve independently in the same way remains a hypothesis unless tested with appropriate comparisons. No human-only or AI-only counterfactual was measured in the featured experiments.
