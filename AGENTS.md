# Portfolio contributor instructions

This is a public portfolio for Troy Vance Williams. Its standard is contact with reality before persuasion.

Read README.md, evidence/STANDARDS.md, and evidence/CLAIM_REGISTER.md before substantive edits. Research repositories retain their scientific and engineering authority; this repository curates public explanations.

Use immutable source links and explicit scopes. Never turn a README assertion into a verified result without checking the supporting artifact. Keep implementation, fresh execution, operational observation, reproduction, hypothesis, architecture, and interpretation distinct.

Attribute material AI involvement. Do not invent human-only authorship, employment history, credentials, deployment metrics, independent review, or frontier-AI impact.

Do not copy private source material, client or personal records, secrets, raw operational traces, or private correspondence. Do not link private sources as though public readers can examine them. Public visibility alone is not permission to amplify sensitive content.

Do not change other repositories, resume stopped experiments, or change repository visibility from portfolio work. Preserve the owning project's stop rules and claim statuses.

For a new case, use templates/CASE_STUDY.md and update the claim register. Check local links and source pins. Report what changed, what was verified, and what remains uncertain. Do not add decorative test counts or badges that imply broader validation than was performed.
