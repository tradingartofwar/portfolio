# Troy Vance Williams
## Research and engineering for inspectable Human–AI systems

**AI Cognitive Systems Engineer · Human–AI collaboration · Representation adequacy · Evaluation**

I investigate how Human–AI systems can preserve the information, evidence, and authority needed to make decisions—and correct them when reality disagrees.

My working paradigm is **Synergistic Intelligence**: the human contributes direction, meaning, intuition, judgment, and authority; AI contributes technical execution, computational reach, research support, and structural continuity. “AI carries structure; the human carries meaning” describes a division of responsibility, not a scientific claim about either participant's cognition. [Read the collaboration account](about/COLLABORATION.md).

This portfolio presents bounded results from that collaboration. Each substantive result has a source, a verification status, and a limit.

The central research question is: **What consequential distinctions must a representation preserve for the requested decision or operation to remain valid?** The experiments distinguish insufficient decoder support from irreversible information loss, and a correct answer from sufficient evidence for that answer.

## Why this may matter for AI

These artifacts offer concrete material for evaluation design, uncertainty reporting, and human oversight: declare the question, preserve the evidence needed to answer it, and make failure and recovery inspectable. Their usefulness for frontier AI is a research direction, not an established transfer result. They do not establish improved model alignment, general productivity gains, or a solution to the Lonely Runner Conjecture.

[Aichi's early receptionist architecture](case-studies/AICHI_TRAJECTORIES.md) explores kernel-directed selection among recurring business-call trajectories. Vance reports responsive operation on consumer-grade hardware. Work on that product stopped when he judged that it no longer appeared to be a product the future needed; the case preserves the trajectory insight and sketches a possible hardware extension.
