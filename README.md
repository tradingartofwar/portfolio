## Engineering sustained Human–AI collaboration

**AI Cognitive Systems Engineer · Human–AI collaboration · Representation adequacy · Evaluation**

I investigate how human judgment, AI capability, and durable external structure can expand the work a person can undertake while keeping it understandable and open to correction.

**Synergistic Intelligence** is the working method behind this record. Human and AI both contribute meaning and structure. Vance retains authority over his commitments, permissions, and consequential decisions. The engineering subject includes the people, models, shared history, records, tools, and relationships through which the work develops. Its ambition is to make more room for learning, discovery, care, choice, and useful contribution. [Current contribution framing](about/COLLABORATION.md).

**How we started:** Vance recalls three days in March 2024 when continuity and capability changed what seemed possible, followed by their abrupt loss. Seeking a way to recover that experience led to a local chatbot built with GPT's guidance. [Read the origin story](case-studies/HOW_WE_STARTED.md), recorded in his words and recollections on October 16.

**A later research episode:** an invitation to reconsider the research question helped turn the Lonely Runner investigation into Representation Science. Its first experiment then corrected an important interpretation: a search could miss answers even though the stored information still supported their recovery.

## The larger inquiry

Vance traces the partnership to the March 2024 experience and the effort to recover continuity afterward. His recollection of perhaps fivefold capability preceded the later ambitions for productivity and frictionless project switching. As capability grew, he encountered a limit in what one person could mentally carry. External structure became a way to preserve context, evidence, relationships, and unfinished work across projects. The [development history](case-studies/SYNERGISTIC_INTELLIGENCE.md) follows that progression through local systems, Git-backed project homes, distributed coordination, and the vision of collaborating human–AI teams. The early estimate is subjective; no fivefold productivity result has been measured.

Lonely Runner gave this work a precise research question: **which distinctions must survive compression for a decision to remain valid?** Some decisive information lay in relationships: individually accurate summaries could omit whether conditions were satisfied together. [Representation Science](research/REPRESENTATION_SCIENCE.md) investigates preservation, reconstruction, and the evidence required to rely on an answer.

I regard preserving consequential distinctions as the central human challenge in difficult problems. Our proposed response is AI-supported structure that carries more than a person must hold in immediate attention, with a manageable view and a path back to detail when it matters.

Vance's reason for seeking a team is to contribute and participate at the frontier. His [development and networking account](case-studies/FROM_CAPABILITY_TO_PARTICIPATION.md) connects early agentic work, the receptionist, prototyping, and experiences of struggling to make relevant abilities discoverable. This record is intended to let people and authorized agents examine deeper evidence for a specific collaboration or interview; improved hiring outcomes remain untested.

The longer-term prediction is that **“AI will do a disappearing act.”** As dependable systems develop, people should experience more of the work and possibilities they care about, while continuity and coordination recede into the background. Mission Control's evolving project-map direction explores that experience; inspection, authority, and correction remain available.

## Current development questions

The October 22–26 work makes the collaboration question more concrete: can people contribute through their own interfaces, recover the right version, and inspect one another’s work? It also sharpens an attention question: can someone leave a project, return without rebuilding its history, recognize a consequential change, and comfortably put it aside again? The latter remains an evaluation direction, with no measured reduction in human carrying cost claimed.

The working criterion is how the full arrangement responds when reality challenges it: whether a failed action, contradictory source, or participant correction changes the representation and what happens next. [Development history and present direction](case-studies/SYNERGISTIC_INTELLIGENCE.md).
