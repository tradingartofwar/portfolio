# Troy Vance Williams
## Engineering sustained Human–AI collaboration

**AI Cognitive Systems Engineer · Human–AI collaboration · Representation adequacy · Evaluation**

I develop ways for human judgment, AI capability, and durable external structure to work together across sustained projects. I investigate what these arrangements make possible, what the human must still carry, and how the work remains open to correction.

**Synergistic Intelligence** is the operating method behind this portfolio. It grew from learning complementary strengths, through the discovery of a human integration bottleneck, into external operating structures and distributed project systems. “AI carries structure; the human carries meaning” expresses the guiding responsibility. [Read the development history](case-studies/SYNERGISTIC_INTELLIGENCE.md) and [contribution account](about/COLLABORATION.md).

The longer-term prediction is that **AI will do a disappearing act**: as dependable systems become established, people will experience less of the machinery and more of the work and possibilities it supports. The current map and coordination work investigate that direction.

This portfolio presents the working method alongside concrete results. Each substantive result has a source, a verification status, and a limit.

Within this collaboration, Representation Science asks: **What consequential distinctions must a representation preserve for the requested decision or operation to remain valid?** The experiments distinguish insufficient decoder support from irreversible information loss, and a correct answer from sufficient evidence for that answer.

## Why this may matter for AI

These artifacts offer concrete material for evaluation design, uncertainty reporting, and human oversight: declare the question, preserve the evidence needed to answer it, and make failure and recovery inspectable. Their usefulness for frontier AI is a research direction, not an established transfer result. They do not establish improved model alignment, general productivity gains, or a solution to the Lonely Runner Conjecture.

[Aichi's early receptionist architecture](case-studies/AICHI_TRAJECTORIES.md) explores kernel-directed selection among recurring business-call trajectories. Vance reports responsive operation on consumer-grade hardware. Work on that product stopped when he judged that it no longer appeared to be a product the future needed; the case preserves the trajectory insight and sketches a possible hardware extension.
